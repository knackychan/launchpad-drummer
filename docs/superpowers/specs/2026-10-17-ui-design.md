Additions to the parent spec that this design introduces (all approved in session):
undo/redo; a MIDI LEARN list in Setup (only way to remove a parameter binding); New/Open

### 2.5 CRT post-process (added 2026-10-17; reworked the same day: glitch and desync removed; pixel mask, glow, curvature, scan line)

Brief from the user: retro, grainy, old CRT, with a pixelated post-process look that makes the
elements glow, a slight fish-eye, and a subtle desync now and then (later replaced by an intermittent scan line); subtle overall. Later: "washed out, breaks readability" — so it must add punch, never fog: colours and elements stand out like the plotter reference.

Three fixed, click-through layers above the console (under the mockup switcher only), kept
as separate elements so each one blends with the console: **bloom** (z 63), **pixel mask**
(z 64), **tube** (z 65: vignette, grain, scan line, bezel). Strength is one factor
`--crt`: OFF 0 / LOW 1 (default) / MEDIUM 1.35, an app setting in SETUP › DISPLAY (per user,
not per set). Values below are for LOW.

| Effect | Recipe |
|---|---|
| Bloom (glow) | `backdrop-filter: brightness(.75) contrast(4) saturate(1.8) blur(5px)` screen-blended at 32 % (capped at 45 %): a hard threshold so only lit pads, active keys, traces and text glow, in a tight halo; dark areas get nothing (the earlier 9 px / 50 % bloom fogged the whole picture) |
| Pixel mask (pixelated look) | a grid of `--px` cells (3 device px, snapped so 125 / 150 % scaling gives no moiré): row gaps 34 %, column gaps 7 %, multiplied at 18 % (capped at 26 %). No RGB triad tint (it muddied the hues) |
| Curvature (fish-eye) | the mask's rows and columns are drawn through a barrel mapping `screen = c + d·(1 − 2k·r²)` (k LOW 0.07, MEDIUM 0.10, d and r relative to the half diagonal; raised one notch on user request) so the scanlines bow outwards like a curved tube; plus rounded screen corners (44 / 38 px), an inner edge shadow and a faint glass reflection top-left. The UI itself is not bent: text stays sharp and clicks land where they look |
| Real bend (optional) | SETUP › DISPLAY › BEND: FLAT (default) / BENT. BENT bulges the picture itself with an SVG `feDisplacementMap` (radial map, 4.5 px at the edge middles, 9 px in the corners) + 0.4 px blur. Trade-offs: glyphs near the edges get softer and slightly broken (the displacement samples nearest-pixel), and what you see is up to 9 px away from where you click near the corners |
| Punch | console `contrast(1 + 0.1 × --crt) saturate(1 + 0.25 × --crt)`: colours stand out, blacks stay black (replaces the earlier brightness lift, which greyed them) |
| Vignette | elliptical, from 66 % out, edges darkened up to 20 %; bezel inner shadow 34 px at 45 % |
| Grain | 240 px grey fractal-noise tile, **overlay** blend at 10 % (never lifts black), jumps to a new offset 10 × per second |
| Flicker | tube layer opacity dips to 93–96 % for one frame, twice every 6 s |
| Scan line | every 7–18 s a thin bright line (28 % white edge over a 40 px pale-blue trail) sweeps down the screen in 1.8–2.7 s; about 45 % of the time it decelerates instead, halts somewhere between 30 % and 70 % of the height, lingers and fades (1.4–2.6 s in all). Replaces the constant roll bar and the desync (user: "remove the desync, keep a scan line that stops mid-course sometimes") |
| Plastic frame | while the CRT layer is on, the console sits inside a 12 px beige plastic frame (`--plastic`): a solid spread around the rounded screen (44 / 38 px), a dark 3 px gasket at the glass with a 1 px highlight, a bevel along the window edge (light top, dark bottom, warm inner shading) and a small green power lamp on the bottom edge. The console gets a 12 px margin (so 24 px less width and height; the Play layout re-solves). OFF removes the frame and the margin |
| Phosphor bloom on text | LCD text glows in its own colour (4 px at 45 %: tighter and brighter) |
| Chromatic fringe | panel legends, brand, active rail key, power-on title, rack titles: ±0.5 px red / cyan text shadows |

Reduced motion (OS setting): mask, grain and vignette stay, still; no flicker or scan line. OFF removes all three layers and the contrast / saturation punch. Nothing in them carries
information.

**Cost (for Plan 1B):** the bloom is one full-window backdrop blur per frame and the
contrast / saturation punch one filtered pass of the console; both are GPU work in WebView2. Measure on
the target laptop; if it costs frame time, LOW can drop the bloom first.

The screen is a **stage** plus a **side drawer**. Bay padding 8 px, gaps 10 px.

```
┌ stage ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┬ tabs ┐
│ [ Launchpad panel ]  10  [ MIXER 8 ch + master ] 10 [TELEMETRY]│ BROW │  (drawer floats here ▸)
│   39 px pads at 1280×720             same height    [ scope   ]│ SER  │
│   + bank row                                        [ spectrum]│ EDIT │
│                        8                            [ orbit   ]│ OR ⇥ │

└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴──────┘
```

- **Telemetry column** (added 2026-10-17, section 4.8): right of the stack, as tall as it.
  Its width is solved after the pads: the widest of 300 … 220 px (10 px steps) that costs
  no pad size; otherwise 220 px if pads stay ≥ 52 px; otherwise the 30 px **folded strip**
  (vertical L/R meters); otherwise (docked drawer on a small window) hidden, and the mixer
  level fills carry the levels. It never takes the narrow mixer's room (the docked-drawer
  rule below still holds). Measured: 1280×720 250 px (pads 39, mixer columns 35);
  1440×900 260 px (pads 47); 1920×1080 with the editor docked 220 px (pads 60, was 70);
  1280×720 with the drawer docked: hidden (pads 36 / columns 33 as before).
- **Stage**: a vertical stack centred in the stage (both ways), 8 px gaps: on top the
  Launchpad panel and mixer side by side, the mixer plate exactly as tall as the Launchpad
  plate; under it the **keys plate** (section 4.6) and the **sequencer** (section 4.7), both
  exactly as wide as that pair. Their left blocks are both 223 px, so the step grid lines
  up with the keybed.
- **Height split** (changed 2026-10-17, "reduce the height of the three modules"):
  `avail = H − 16 − lpChrome(87) − 7·gap − 2·8`. The sequencer asks for
  `clamp(avail × 0.25, 110, 320)`; pads and keybed share the rest (keybed ≈ 0.9 × pad,
  44–110 px); the sequencer then takes whatever pads and keys leave, up to 360 px. A
  folded keys plate or sequencer costs 28 px and gives the rest back to the pads.

  | Window | Pads | Mixer col | Keybed | Sequencer | Telemetry |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1280×720 | 39 | 35 | 44 | 130 | 250 |
  | 1280×720, drawer docked | 36 | 33 | 44 | 154 | hidden |
  | 1440×900 | 47 | 42 | 44 | 154 | 260 |
  | 1440×900, drawer docked | 42 | 38 | 44 | 194 | 30 (strip) |
  | 1920×1080, editor docked (default) | 60 | 54 | 50 | 220 | 220 |

  viewport is a little smaller than the window; the earlier 1440×900 row said 55 px pads,
  the old mockup also gives 47 px under these conditions). The mixer columns now get only
  what pads and telemetry leave, still ≥ 0.9 × pad when the pads are width-limited.

  At 1280×720 with the sequencer folded pads are 51 px; with keys and sequencer both folded
  55 px.
- **Side drawer** (300 px): shows the sample **browser** or the **pad editor**, one at a
  time, chosen by two vertical tabs at the right edge (28 px). Click a tab to open, click it
  again, press Esc or Ctrl+B / Ctrl+E to close. Pressing a pad never opens it (that would
  cover the mixer while playing); the one automatic open is SHOW on a missing-sample notice.
  - **Docked** (default at every width, changed 2026-10-17 after the user saw the floating
    drawer crop the mixer): the drawer is a third column and the stage shrinks; pads go down
    to 36 px and the mixer to narrow columns (section 6) so nothing is hidden. Measured:
    1280×720 pads 36 / mixer columns 33 px; 1442×750 42 / 45 px.
  - **Floating** (⇥ key under the tabs toggles; also the fallback when even a 30 px-column
    mixer would not fit): the drawer slides over the right end of the mixer. Pads stay

  - Starts open on the editor on windows from 1780 px wide; closed otherwise.
- **Why not three always-open columns:** browser + Launchpad + mixer + editor need about
  1750 px; the minimum window is 1280. Pads would fall to the floor, so the two drawer
  panels share one slot.

### 4.1 Launchpad panel

- Top row: 8 round buttons as on the hardware, 22 px. Only the last (Mixer, CC 111) is live =
  **STOP ALL** (tooltip "Stop all · Esc"); others dim, not focusable.
- 8×8 rubber pads, live LED colours. **Pad size** = `floor(clamp(36px, min(fit-width,
  fit-height), 112px))`, gap 5 px. The width limit is shared with the mixer, see
  section 6: pads and mixer columns grow together, so neither looks squished. The height
  limit is shared with the keys and the sequencer (height split in section 4; sizes in the
  table there). Plate padding 8 px, row gaps 6 px. The 7 dim hardware-mirror buttons above
  the grid cost ~3 px per pad; kept for fidelity.
- Pad label: sample name, uppercase, 8.5 px at small pads scaling to 12 px (`0.145 × pad`);
  at 39 px most names truncate to ~5 characters (the editor shows the full name),
  white 78 % with dark shadow, bottom-left, truncated. Empty pad: unlit, faint `+` on hover. Missing sample: blinks red (from LED
  state) + `!` glyph.
- **Mouse**: press = play **and** select; release ends a hold-mode pad. Right-click menu:
  Learn MIDI note…, Clear pad, Show sample in Explorer.
- **Hardware pad hit** also selects that pad on screen.
- Selection = 2 px white bezel, 3 px offset.
- Side buttons + **scribble strips** (66 px LCD, 14 px shorter than a pad row): bank name; active
  bank bright, empty banks dim, red dot if the bank has missing samples. Click = switch.
  Double-click (or F2) = rename inline, max 16 chars, Enter commits, Esc cancels.
  Hardware side button switches the screen too.

### 4.2 Bank strip (last row of the Launchpad plate, under the grid, hairline above)

### 4.7 Sequencer (step grid, bottom of the stack)

Built in the mockup 2026-10-17 (first draft, user review pending). Replaces the reserved
dock: it now sits **under the keys plate**, same width, open by default.

```
┌ sequencer ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [▶][●] BPM [100] SWING [50%] │ STEPS [4|8|16|32] [+ LANE] [CLEAR]        [▾] │
│ ▌[1·KICK_808    ] M × │ ██ ░░ ░░ ░░ ▓▓ ░░ ▒▒ ░░ ▓▓ ░░ ▒▒ ░░ ▓▓ ░░ ░░ ░░ │
│ ▌[1·SNARE_808   ] M × │ ▓▓ ░░ ░░ ░░ ██ ░░ ░░ ░░ ▓▓ ░░ ░░ ░░ ██ ░░ ░░ ░░ │
│ ▌[1·HAT_CLOSED  ] M × │ ▒▒ ░░ ▒▒ ░░ ██ ░░ ▒▒ ░░ ▒▒ ░░ ▒▒ ░░ ██ ░░ ▒▒ ░░ │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  ██ accent   ▒▒ on   ▓▓ / ░░ off (beat start lighter)   orange outline = playhead
```

- **Model**: a **step grid, one lane per pad** (bank + pad, so a pattern can mix banks). A
  lane plays its pad through the normal pad path, so the Launchpad LEDs flash, mix groups,
  choke and humanize apply, and hold-mode pads are released after 90 % of a step.
  One pattern per set, saved with the set, covered by undo (steps, lanes, length).
- **Header** (22 px; left block 223 px like the keys): PLAY/STOP round key (green ■ while
  playing); REC round key ● (red when armed); `BPM` LCD 60–200 and `SWING` LCD 50–75 %
  (drag up/down, wheel, arrows, double-click resets to 100 / 50 %); `STEPS` switch
  4 | 8 | 16 | 32 (growing fills the new steps with repeats of the old length if they are
  empty; shrinking hides steps but keeps them); `+ LANE` adds the selected pad (refused with
  a notice if empty or already there); `CLEAR` empties all steps (undoable); fold ▾.
- **Record (REC)**: arming REC starts playback (one undo step for the whole take). Every
  pad you play while it runs (screen, Launchpad, computer keys, learned Keystation notes)
  is written to the **nearest step** (this one, or the next if the hit is more than half a
  step late, in which case the sequencer does not fire it again on that pass). A pad
  without a lane gets one. Accents are kept; recording never removes steps. Disarm with ●
  again; stopping keeps REC armed for the next run.
- **Lanes**: colour bar in the pad colour, LCD `bank·SAMPLE` (bright when it is the selected
  pad; click = go to that bank and select the pad), `M` mute (amber), `×` remove. A lane
  whose pad was cleared shows `EMPTY` in dim italics and stays silent.
- **Steps**: 4, 8, 16 or 32 cells sharing the width, 2 px gaps, every 4th cell lighter (beat).
  Click = on/off, right-click or Shift+click = accent (velocity 127 vs 90), drag paints the
  same value across cells. A step switched on while stopped auditions the pad.
- **Playhead**: orange outline on the current column; lit cells under it brighten. Esc /
  STOP ALL also stops the sequencer.
- **Height**: see section 4. Lanes share the body height, 14–24 px each; under 14 px the
  body scrolls (at 1280×720: 5 lanes fit, a 6th scrolls).
- **Fold**: ▾ folds to a 28 px strip `▸ SEQUENCER  5 lanes · 16 steps · 100 BPM`.
- **Engine (Plan 1B)**: the sequencer clock runs in the Rust router, sample-accurate, not in
  the UI; the UI only edits the pattern and draws the playhead from ~30 Hz position events.
- **Still open**: several patterns per set (slots A–H to switch verse / chorus) or a song
  chain; per-step velocity beyond accent; count-in before recording; keyboard shortcuts for
  play / rec / fold; relation to the Part 4 looper (audio loops); whether the sequencer
  ships in Part 1.

### 4.8 Telemetry (music-reactive instruments)

Added 2026-10-17. A column of glass screens right of the stack (width and fallbacks in
section 4), legend `TELEMETRY`. Visual only: `aria-hidden`, no controls except fold. All of
it shows the **master output** or **channel activity**, never decoration; silent audio =
still screens.

```
┌ TELEMETRY ─────────────────────┐
│ (SIGNAL) T+01:12.4 ▪▪▫▫     [▸] │  head: pill, session clock, 4 beat lamps, fold
│ OSCILLOSCOPE         2.7 ms/div │
│ ┌──┬──┬──┬──┼──┬──┬──┬──┐      │  8×6 grid, red centre crosshair, green trace
│ └──┴──┴──┴──┼──┴──┴──┴──┘      │
│ SPECTRUM            PEAK 94 Hz  │  28 log bands, segmented, peak caps
│ ORBIT       STEP 11/16 · 100 BPM│  channel radar inside, step ring + playhead outside
│ MASTER L / R        PK −3.1 dB  │  segmented peak meters
└────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Head**: orange pill `SIGNAL`; clock `T+mm:ss.t` since the app started; 4 beat lamps
  (quarter notes of the running sequencer, the downbeat lamp cream, others orange); fold ▸
  (folds to the 30 px strip; click the strip to open again).
- **Oscilloscope**: master waveform, ~21 ms window (1024 samples at 48 kHz), triggered on a
  rising zero crossing so periodic sounds stand still; trace `--phos` 1.5 px with glow and
  3 fading afterglow traces (none with reduced motion); grid `--vec` at 13 %, centre
  crosshair red at 50 %; readouts `CH1 · MASTER`, `x ms/div`. Silent: flat trace +
  `STANDBY · PLAY A PAD`.
- **Spectrum**: 28 log bands 40 Hz–16 kHz from a 2048-point FFT; 3 px segments with 1 px
  gaps, teal below 60 % of the height, amber to 85 %, red above; bars fall ~12 % per frame,
  cream peak caps fall slowly; scale `100 / 1k / 10k`; readout `PEAK f` (loudest bin).
- **Orbit**: inside, an 8-spoke radar of the mixer channels (each point's radius = channel
  energy, dots in the channel's colour, orange polygon); outside, a ring with one tick per
  sequencer step (beats longer, steps that have hits amber) and the **playhead planet**
  (cream dot, orange tail) moving smoothly between steps. Stopped: planet parked at the top,
  readout `IDLE · BPM`.
- **Master L / R**: 36-segment peak meters −48…+3 dB (green < −9, amber to 0, red above),
  falling 36 dB/s, 1.2 s peak hold; scale −40 / −24 / −12 / −6 / 0; readout `PK x dB`.
- **Channel energy** (radar, mixer fills, top-strip chips): a hit sets its channel to
  `velocity/127 × fader gain` (keys routed to a channel count too), decaying with a 110 ms
  half-life. In the app this should come from real per-channel peaks (section 7.3).
- **Raster look** (added 2026-10-17, ref `docs/reference/Web-design/18b709cd…jpg`, a vector
  CRT plotter): every module draws into a low-res buffer (1 raster pixel = 2 CSS px), which is
  then **bit-crushed**: 4 levels per colour channel, 4 alpha levels (faint lines snap to
  visible), every other raster row at 55 % so traces, bars and text read as coarse
  horizontal scan stripes. It is blown up nearest-neighbour, with a 2.2 px blurred copy
  underneath as phosphor glow. Labels inside the screens use a 3×5 bitmap font stamped after
  the crush (so they stay legible); module headers and readouts use VT323 (amber titles,
  green values, both glowing). Scope: solid cyan frame and verticals, dashed horizontals,
  solid red vertical centre, dashed red horizontal centre, near-white trace with green
  afterglow. Spectrum segments are 4 px with 2 px gaps.
- Frame rate: the screens redraw at display rate (~60 Hz) while Play is shown; the strip,
  chips and fills keep running elsewhere only where visible.

## 5. Drag and drop

Sources: Explorer files, browser files, pads. Targets: pads, pad editor (= selected pad),
side buttons / scribble strips (hover only).

| Drag | Drop on | Result | Target tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Explorer/browser file | empty pad | copy to `samples/` (clash → `-2`, `-3`…); defaults: row colour, 0 dB, 0 st, ONE-SHOT, no choke, mix = row | IMPORT |
| Explorer/browser file | filled pad | replace sample, reset trim; keep colour, volume, pitch, mode, choke, mix | REPLACE |
| several files | pad | target pad, then next **empty** pads in reading order (right, then the row above); leftovers dropped with notice "n files not imported — bank full" | IMPORT ×n |
| pad | empty pad | move (all params) | MOVE |
| pad | filled pad | swap | SWAP |
| pad + Ctrl | any pad | copy (overwrites a filled target) | COPY |
| anything | side button / scribble strip held 400 ms | switch to that bank, drag continues | — |
| non-audio file or folder | anywhere | not-allowed cursor, red bezel on hovered pad, notice "Not an audio file" | — |
| anything | outside targets, or Esc | cancel (no drag-to-delete) | — |

Rules: 4 px threshold (below = click). A pad drag starts after the press has sounded; hold
voices are released when the drag starts. Feedback: ghost = small pad in source colour +
name; target gets a dashed white bezel + LCD tag. MIDI-learn bindings follow the sound on
move/swap; copy does not duplicate the binding. Every drop = one undo step (an undone import
leaves the copied file in `samples/`).

**Implementation constraint:** Tauri 2 intercepts native file drops, so Explorer drops come
from the window drag-drop event (paths + position, hit-tested against pad rects). Internal
drags use pointer events, not HTML5 DnD.

## 6. Mixer (on the Play screen)

There is no MIX screen: the mixer is always visible on Play, right of the Launchpad panel
(decided 2026-10-17). A Launch Control XL drawn as a plate: label column (30 px), 8 group
columns, bezel gap, MASTER column.

**Sizing (rules, not fixed numbers).** The plate is **always exactly as tall as the Launchpad
plate** and sits next to it. The fader is the stretchy part: `fader = launchpadPlate − (rows +
gaps + padding)`, min 110 px; when the plate is too short for that, the knobs shrink first
(down to 24 px). With the sequencer open the fader is ~110 px at 1280×720 and ~330 px at
1920×1080. Width: pad size and
column width solve together, `8·pad + 9·column + 76 ≤ stage width` with `column ≈ 0.9 × pad`;
when height limits the pads first, the leftover width widens the columns (30–110 px; under
48 px only with the drawer docked on small windows). Everything inside scales with the
column: knobs `0.6 × column` (20–60 px), key height `0.36 × column` (26–38), LCD text
8.5–13 px, fader 22–64 px wide. Under 44 px columns MUTE / SOLO read `M` / `S` and group
names truncate in the scribble strip. Build it as a standalone
component taking `columnWidth` and `height` props (BAND reuses it).

**Channel inputs** (added 2026-10-17). The 8 channels are buses, not fixed drum groups:
- **Pads** join a channel through their MIX GROUP (pad editor), as before.
- **Keys** (the keys layer, section 4.6) go to MASTER or to exactly one channel. Routed to
  a channel, the keys follow its fader, pan, pitch, low-pass, mute and solo, so the LCXL
  controls them like any group. A channel can carry pads and keys together.
- **From the mixer**: click a channel's scribble strip (or right-click) = menu
  `Pads: n in group k (pad editor › MIX GROUP)` (info) · `✓ Keys: E.PIANO` (toggle; shows
  where the keys are now if elsewhere) · `Rename… F2`. Double-click still renames.
- **From the keys plate**: the output LCD (`→ MASTER` / `→ 8 VOX`), same choice.
- A channel carrying the keys shows a dot in the keys sound's colour before its name; the
  tooltip lists its inputs. Renaming the channel (e.g. "KEYS") is up to the user.
- Later inputs (Part 4 instruments, a second keyboard, audio in) use the same rule: each
  input picks MASTER or one channel.

Per column, top to bottom: scribble LCD `n NAME` (9 px, double-click / F2 rename, max 12
chars, stored uppercase, ellipsis when longer than the column) · PAN knob 30 px (`L50…C…R50`)
· PITCH knob (±12 st) · LOW-PASS knob (200 Hz–20 kHz log, 20 kHz = `OFF`) · fader (−∞…+6 dB,
0 dB at 75 % travel) · dB LCD (number only, unit printed once as the row label `dB`) ·
MUTE (amber when on) · SOLO (green when on). MASTER: fader + LCD only. Row labels (silk, left):
GROUP, PAN, PITCH, LPF, the volume scale (+6 / 0 / −12 / −40 / −∞ printed once, in the label
column, not per fader), dB, MUTE, SOLO.

**Level fills** (added 2026-10-17 with the telemetry): a 3 px bar right of each fader slot
rises with the channel's level on the fader's own scale (green to 0 dB, amber to +4, red
above; drawn under the fader cap). MASTER has two (L left of the slot, R right) from the
master peak meters.

While the side drawer floats it covers the right end of the mixer (MASTER and the last
groups). Close it (tab, Esc) to use them; the two never need to be used at once.

Fader law (UI ↔ dB): `p ≥ 0.75: dB = (p − 0.75)/0.25 × 6`; `p < 0.75: dB = 40·log10(p/0.75)`;
`p = 0: −∞`.

Hardware LCXL moves update the screen at ~30 Hz. A screen change leaves the hardware control
out of step until it next moves, then the value **jumps** to the hardware (no pickup).

### 7.2 Power-on LCD

Shown in the centre bay (rail and status strip stay) on first run, when the last set folder

("Couldn't open {name} — set.json and backup are unreadable. Files left untouched.").
Content: title, status line, lamps LAUNCHPAD / LCXL / AUDIO, keys NEW SET (folder picker,

### 7.3 Backend requirements implied by the UI (for Plan 1B)

- Router reports save errors (for notice 4 and the save LED).
- Router emits "dropout happened" events with a timestamp (10 s amber window).
- Undo history lives in the UI or router; covers set edits: pad params, sample replace,
  move/swap/copy, clear, bank names, light modes, humanize, group names, learn bindings.
  **Not** mixer values (from screen or LCXL).
- Audition plays a file once through the master bus, not through a pad.
- Sequencer: pattern (lanes = bank + pad, 32 step values, length, BPM, swing, mutes) is part
  of `set.json`; the router runs the clock and fires pad hits like a hardware hit
  (LEDs, choke, groups); it sends the step position to the UI at ~30 Hz.
- Keys plate: MIDI note on/off from the KEYS port goes to a polyphonic SoundFont
  instrument (current preset, own volume, then the master bus or the chosen mixer channel,
  stored in `set.json`); learned notes still go to
  their pad. Pitch bend ±2 st and mod wheel (vibrato) apply to held notes. The router
  forwards note on/off and wheel values to the UI for the mirror.
- Sequencer record: the router timestamps live pad hits against the running clock and
  writes them to the nearest step (same rule as section 4.7), then sends the change to the
  UI and to undo.
- **Telemetry stream** (new, section 4.8): the audio thread keeps lock-free taps of the
  master bus; the router sends the UI, ~60 Hz while Play is visible: the last 2048 master
  samples (mono sum, or decimated) for the scope, 28 band levels (or the raw FFT
  magnitudes), master L / R peak since the last frame, and per-channel peak since the last
  frame (8 values, post-fader). Nothing is sent while the window is hidden. The mockup
  stands in with WebAudio analysers; it must never cost audio-thread time beyond the copy.

## 10. Keyboard

Plain letters and digits are reserved for playing pads.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `1–8`, `Q–I`, `A–K`, `Z–,` | play bottom 4 rows (row 4 → row 1); Play screen only, never while typing; also selects the pad |
| Space | grid focused or nothing focused: play selected pad; browser: audition |
| Enter (browser) | load file into selected pad |
| Arrows | grid: move selection; knob/fader: change value (Shift = fine) |
| PageUp / PageDown | previous / next bank |
| Alt+1–8 | go to bank |
| F2 | rename active bank, or the focused mixer group when focus is in the mixer |
| Delete | clear selected pad (undoable) |
| Ctrl+B / Ctrl+E | open / close the side drawer on the browser / on the pad editor |
| Ctrl+K | fold / unfold the keys plate |
| Ctrl+1 / Ctrl+2 / Ctrl+, | PLAY / TRAIN (inactive in Part 1) / SETUP |
| Ctrl+N / Ctrl+O | new set / open set |
| Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z | undo / redo |
| Shift+F10, Menu key | context menu of the focused control |
| Esc | cancel innermost (drag → learn → rename → menu/popover); if nothing, STOP ALL |

## 11. Accessibility basics

- Focus ring 2 px `--focus`, 2 px offset, `:focus-visible` only, on every control.
- Grid: `role="grid"`, one Tab stop, arrows inside, `aria-activedescendant`. Pad label
  "Row 4, column 2, KICK_808, mix 4, choke off" / "…, empty" / "…, sample missing".
- Knobs/faders `role="slider"` with `aria-valuetext` ("−3.0 dB"). Latching keys
  `aria-pressed`. Choke/mix/mode rows `radiogroup`. Mode rail `tablist`; blank caps
  `aria-disabled` + label "Games, coming in Part 3". Browser `role="tree"` with
  `aria-activedescendant`.
- Notice LCD `aria-live` (polite; assertive for errors).
- Never colour alone: lamps have printed labels + state tooltips; missing pad shows `!`;
  MUTE/SOLO printed; Train notes carry L/R glyphs.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no key transitions, no toast slide, on-screen missing-sample
  blink becomes steady red + `!`, learn blink becomes steady; CRT layers still (no grain
  motion, flicker or scan line); no scope afterglow. Hardware
  unaffected; LED mirror and telemetry values still update.
- Telemetry and the CRT layer are `aria-hidden` and click-through; nothing in them is the
  only place a state shows (levels also reach the mixer fills, step position the sequencer).
- Contrast, black-base tokens (WCAG, computed, CRT off): amber labels on panel 10.6:1, cream
  LCD on glass 19.5:1, dim LCD on glass 8.2:1 / on panel 7.6:1, key text 10.3:1, cyan legends
  on body 10.6:1, dark text on orange 6.3:1. The CRT vignette (up to 20 % at the edges) and
  pixel mask lower these slightly near the window edges. On the cobalt body: amber labels 7.8:1,
  dim LCD 5.6:1, cyan legends 7.5:1; on navy panels: amber 9.9:1, dim 7.1:1; key text 9.4:1. BENT softens edge text further, one reason it is off by default.
- Follows Windows display scaling (Tauri).
